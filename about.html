Frequently Asked Questions

Ceramic coating is a liquid nano-polymer that chemically bonds with the factory paint to provide extreme gloss, hydrophobicity, and chemical resistance against bird droppings and sap. PPF (Paint Protection Film) is a physical, self-healing polyurethane film that offers heavy-duty protection against rock chips, deep scratches, and physical damage.

A standard premium exterior and interior detail takes about 4-8 hours. Graphene or Ceramic coating usually takes 1-2 days due to the intensive paint correction and IR curing time required. Full body Grace Shield PPF installation can take 3-5 days to ensure perfect edge-wrapping and curing.

Yes, absolutely. All our Ceramic Coating and PPF packages come with an official, written warranty ranging from 3 to 7 years depending on the tier selected.

We recommend washing the car every 2 weeks using a pH-neutral shampoo and the two-bucket method. Avoid automated brush car washes as they cause micro-scratches.
