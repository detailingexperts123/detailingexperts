Premium Ceramic Coating for Long-Lasting Paint Protection

Sacrificial Barrier Engineering

Ceramic Coating is a premium liquid protective coating that chemically bonds with your vehicle's factory paint to create a durable, transparent layer of protection. Unlike traditional waxes or sealants, it delivers long-lasting gloss, excellent water repellency and resistance against UV rays, bird droppings, tree sap and everyday environmental contaminants.

At Detailing Experts, every Ceramic Coating application begins with thorough paint preparation and correction to ensure the coating bonds perfectly with the surface. Maintenance Guidelines

The Two-Bucket Clean Method: Use separate wash and rinse bucket pairs to isolate sand and abrasive debris particles effectively.
pH Neutral Shampoo Blends: Clean your vehicle using safe car shampoos that are completely free from heavy waxes or harsh acidic silicon additives.
Avoid Automatic Car Washes: Avoid stiff mechanical rotating spin brushes that carry a high risk of introducing micro-marring and surface swirls.

Industry Myths vs Facts

Myth: Ceramic coatings protect panels from high-speed rock chips. Fact: They excel at resisting fine micro-swirls and environmental oxidation. Preventing fast, high-impact stone damage requires an application of Grace Shield Paint Protection Film (PPF).
Myth: Coated vehicles never require manual washing ever again. Fact: While the topcoat becomes highly hydrophobic and self-cleaning, regular gentle hand wash sessions remain vital to remove road film and preserve maximum gloss.
