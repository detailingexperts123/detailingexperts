Deep Cabin Restoration & Care

Revitalizing Every Inch of Your Interior

Professional Interior Car Detailing goes far beyond a regular car wash. It is an intensive deep-cleaning and restoration service that revives upholstery, conditions luxury leather surfaces, and sanitizes ventilation channels to remove accumulated dust, bacteria, and allergens.

Using localized high-pressure steam and pH-neutral cleaners, our technicians carefully clean delicate trims, instrument clusters, and carpets without leaving behind greasy residues or unpleasant odours.

Complete Service Checklist

A systematic breakdown of everything included during our professional interior detailing treatments.

Deep Vacuum Extraction

High-suction vacuum extraction removes dust, sand, embedded debris, and food particles from carpets, seams, seat gaps, and boot spaces.

Dashboard Restoration

Thorough cleaning of instrument clusters, touch screens, AC vents, and consoles using non-greasy, anti-static interior cleaners.

Leather Conditioning

Safe cleansing of luxury leather seating followed by premium aloe-enriched conditioners to restore suppleness and prevent cracking.

Fabric & Carpet Shampoo

Deep extraction shampooing lifts stubborn stains, mud marks, and liquid spills from fabric upholstery, roof liners, and floor mats.

Steam Sanitization

High-temperature steam treatment applied to AC vents, cup holders, and high-touch areas to kill germs and eliminate bacteria.

Odour Decomposition

Molecular neutralizing treatments eliminate lingering tobacco smoke, pet odours, and damp smells, leaving the cabin smelling fresh.

Our Detailing Process

Every vehicle follows a standardized multi-stage workflow executed by trained professionals.

01

Inspection & Dry Vacuum

Detailed assessment of fabric wear and stains followed by compressed air blowing and deep vacuuming across all cabin zones.

02

Steam & Scrubbing

Delicate scrubbing of door trims, consoles, and steering wheels accompanied by high-pressure steam sanitization.

03

Seat & Carpet Extraction

Shampoo application and machine extraction for fabric seats, or dedicated leather cleansing for leather interiors.

04

Protection & Dressing

UV-protective dressing applied to vinyl and plastics along with glass polishing for streak-free optical clarity.
