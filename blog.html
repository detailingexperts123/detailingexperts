Guide 01 · Paint Protection

PPF vs Ceramic Coating: What Does Your Car Actually Need?

PPF and ceramic coating solve different protection problems. PPF is a physical film installed over selected painted surfaces, while ceramic coating forms a protective layer over the surface. The right choice depends on how the vehicle is used, what areas need protection and the finish you want.

When PPF makes more sense

PPF is particularly useful on areas exposed to road debris and physical impact. Owners who drive frequently on highways or want stronger protection against stone chips may consider PPF for high-impact areas such as the front bumper, bonnet, fenders and mirrors.

When ceramic coating makes more sense

A ceramic coating is focused more on surface protection, gloss, easier maintenance and water behaviour. It can be a useful choice when your priority is maintaining a clean, glossy finish and simplifying routine washing.

Important: PPF and ceramic coating are not interchangeable products. Depending on the vehicle and protection goals, they can also be used together.

Guide 02 · Ceramic Coating

How to Maintain a Ceramic-Coated Car

A ceramic coating does not make a vehicle maintenance-free. Good washing and drying habits are important if you want to preserve the appearance and performance of the coated surface.

Use safe washing methods

  • Use clean wash media and automotive-safe shampoo.
  • Minimise contact with heavily contaminated paint before the main wash.
  • Use suitable drying towels and avoid dragging dirt across the surface.
  • Follow the coating manufacturer's maintenance recommendations.

Don't judge coating health by water behaviour alone

Water beading can change with contamination, maintenance products and environmental conditions. A professional inspection is more useful than assuming a coating has failed simply because beading looks different.

Guide 03 · Paint Correction

Paint Correction vs Polishing: What Is the Difference?

Polishing can refer to a range of paint-refinement processes. Paint correction is a more deliberate process of assessing paint defects, selecting an appropriate combination of machine, pad and compound, and refining the finish while managing how much clear coat is removed.

Why inspection comes first

Swirl marks, oxidation, scratches and previous repair work do not all require the same approach. A proper inspection helps determine what can realistically be improved and what should be left rather than aggressively chased.

Correction should be followed by protection

Once the paint has been refined, the surface can be protected according to the owner's goals. Options can include a ceramic coating or PPF for selected areas.

Guide 04 · Car Care

Premium Car Wash vs Regular Wash: Why the Difference Matters

Washing a car is not simply about removing visible dirt. Technique, wash media, product choice and drying all influence how much friction the paint experiences during routine maintenance.

What a careful wash should focus on

  • Removing loose contamination before aggressive contact.
  • Using appropriate automotive cleaning products.
  • Reducing unnecessary friction during washing.
  • Drying the vehicle with suitable, clean materials.

Especially important for protected vehicles

Vehicles with PPF or ceramic coatings still need proper maintenance. A protection package works best when the routine care around it is appropriate for the product and the manufacturer's recommendations.
